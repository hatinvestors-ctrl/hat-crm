Negotiation Techniques Embedded in the AI

Anchoring
First number sets the reference point. The AI calculates a precise anchor — not a round number — to signal serious analysis and frame the seller's expectations downward from the start.
Reciprocal Concessions
Every concession we make is scripted to get something in return. The plan sequences what we give up (closing date flexibility, inspection waiver, earnest money) to extract price reductions.
Deadline Effect
Motivated sellers respond to urgency. The AI identifies when to introduce a soft deadline ("we have another property under review") based on the seller's motivation score and DOM.
Bracketing
Offer ladder is constructed so our target (MAO) falls in the middle of the negotiation range — giving the seller the psychological satisfaction of "winning" something while we land at our number.
Condition Discovery
Phone script includes three qualifying questions designed to uncover unreported motivation: timeline pressure, estate situations, financial distress — information that shifts our leverage before an offer is made.
Credibility Signaling
Every communication positions HAT as an experienced, fast-closing cash buyer — not a wholesaler. The AI writes in a tone that signals certainty and competence, which reduces seller hesitation.

03

MAO — Maximum Allowable Offer

MAO is the cornerstone number in every deal. It answers the question: what is the most we can pay and still make money?

MAO = (ARV × 75%) − Renovation Cost − $2,450
ARV × 75%   ← investor's ceiling: ARV at a 25% discount
− Renovation Cost  ← what it will cost to make the property retail-ready
− $2,450        ← fixed closing/title/transfer costs (FL market)

The $2,450 accounts for Florida-specific closing costs: doc stamps (0.35%), intangible tax (0.2%), and standard title fees.

Deal Score — 100-Point System

Every lead receives a 0–100 score — a weighted sum of six independently-evaluated categories, each with a defined rubric. Server-computed — the AI explains scores but never invents them.

30 / 30
Deal Return
Best of BRRRR cash-left-in or Flip net profit at MAO. <$30K cash in = GREAT (30 pts), >$60K flip profit = STRONG (30 pts).
20 / 20
Price Gap
Distance between asking price and MAO, in fixed brackets. Ask ≤ MAO = 20 pts. Ask 50%+ above = 1 pt.
15 / 15
Seller Signals
DOM + price drops (base) + AI adds: +5 estate/probate, +4 as-is, +3 motivated language. Capped at 15.
15 / 15
Market & Exit
ZIP-tier score (pre-indexed: 32205/32216=8 pts, 32208=4 pts) plus ARV confidence (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW).
10 / 10
Cash Flow
BRRRR monthly cash flow at MAO. >$400/mo=10pts, >$200/mo=8pts, breakeven=2pts, negative=0.
10 / 10
Data Quality
−4 if reno unknown, −2 if no ARV, −2 if no property details, −2 if no notes. Incentivizes filling in details.

Smart Offer Algorithm AI

The starting offer uses a negotiation-room model — not a fixed percentage. Equal room left on both sides so negotiations meet in the middle at MAO. Credibility floor prevents insulting offers. Seller motivation adjusts both.

Anchor = MAO − (Gap × Room Factor)
Room Factor = 50% baseline − 2% per motivation point (min 20%)
Floor = Ask × (80% − 0.8% per motivation point, min 72%)
Final = max(Anchor, Floor), capped at MAO × 99.5%
